When a spell summons several creatures at once and you name them one at a time,
the naming panel now has an explicit Confirm button (above Skip) to move on to
the next creature. It appears only while another creature is still queued, so
moving focus off the name field - to Skip, the settings gear, or elsewhere - no
longer accidentally names or advances past a creature. Naming a single summon is
unchanged (no Confirm button).
