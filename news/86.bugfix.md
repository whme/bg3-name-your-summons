Local split-screen co-op now tells the players apart. The naming panel opens
for the player who summoned the creature (previously it always opened on player
one's side), and each player's saved names are private to the summons they
currently control - players' name lists no longer mix, even when characters
share a name. When a player leaves the game, their character's summon names
become visible to whoever then controls it (the host). Every player can name
summons and open the settings panel at the same time, independently.

Names now save as you type them rather than only when the name field loses
focus, so a rename is no longer lost when you leave the field without an
explicit confirm.
