Local split-screen co-op now tells the two players apart. The naming panel
opens for the player who summoned the creature (previously it always opened on
player one's side), and each player's saved names are private to the summons
they currently control - the two players' name lists no longer mix, even when
both characters share a name. When the second player leaves the game, their
character's summon names become visible to the host. Both players can name
summons and open the settings panel at the same time, independently.

Names now save as you type them rather than only when the name field loses
focus, so a rename is no longer lost when you leave the field without an
explicit confirm.
