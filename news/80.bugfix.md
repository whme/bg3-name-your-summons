When a spell summons several creatures at once and you name them one at a time,
the naming panel now has an explicit Confirm button (above Skip). On a
controller, moving focus off the name field no longer commits the (default) name
or advances to the next creature, so you can navigate to Skip or the settings
gear without accidentally naming a creature - the Confirm button does that now.
On keyboard the button is an alternative to Enter, which still works as before.
Naming a single summon is unchanged (no Confirm button).
