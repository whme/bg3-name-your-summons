A summon's name in the Examine panel now starts as plain, non-editable text and
only becomes an editable field once you click it. Story-bound summons (such as
"Us" or "Shovel") stay plain text and cannot be renamed there while the "Allow
renaming story-bound summons" setting is off, matching the game's own Examine
panel. With that setting on, story-bound summons are prompted on summon like any
other summon, regardless of the creature-type filter.
